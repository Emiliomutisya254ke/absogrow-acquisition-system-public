# Outreach and sales

## First email — structure
```
Subject: <the single strongest, verifiable problem, naming their domain>
         e.g. "example-hotel.co.ke takes about 8 seconds to show on a phone"

Hello <Business> team,

I looked at / ran <domain> through Google's PageSpeed test for phones on <weekday date>.
<Finding 1: what was seen, with a number>. <Why it costs them customers, in their world>.

Two other things from the same visit: <finding 2>, and <finding 3>.

I run Absogrow AI, a Nairobi digital agency. I can send you the full report with the
specific fixes at no cost. If you want help carrying them out, we can talk after you have seen it.

Shall I send it over?

If you would prefer not to hear from me again, reply "stop" and I will not write again.
[Gmail signature: logo, name, title, phone/WhatsApp, Instagram]
```
Rules: dated measurements (never "today"); shares not exact counts for screen-dependent things; every claim re-checked on the live site before sending; no prices; no invented urgency.

## Follow-ups (closer-sales-playbook style)
Sent as a reply in the same thread ("Re: <first subject>"), only to those who have not answered, after a new inbox check. Each opens on the gap (what the customer wants vs what is in the way), teaches one thing, ends with one question.

## Call script (for businesses already emailed)
| Step | What to say |
|---|---|
| 1. Open | "Hi, this is Emilio from Absogrow AI in Nairobi. I emailed on [date] about your website. Could I speak to whoever looks after your marketing or the website? It'll take two minutes, and if it's not useful, no problem at all." |
| 2. Name the gap | "When I checked [website] on a phone, [one problem from the call list]. Most people searching on their phone leave before that, so some bookings go elsewhere. Have you noticed that?" |
| 3. Ask, then listen | "Where do most of your new [guests / patients / customers] come from right now? What have you tried online so far?" Let them talk; note answers. |
| 4. One clear offer | "I put together a short report on [business] with the specific fixes. Can I send it to you, or WhatsApp it to this number? It's free, and if you want help with it we can talk after you've read it." |
| 5. If yes | Confirm email/WhatsApp and a follow-up day. Status: Interested / Meeting booked. |
| 6. If "not now" / "no" | Thank them, ask if it's OK to check back in a few months. If they ask not to be contacted: Do not contact + suppression list. |
| Rules | Only mention problems in the call list. No discounts, no prices, no invented deadlines. If asked the price: it depends on what they choose after the report. |

## Opener for businesses with no website (strong Google reviews)
"Hi, this is Emilio from Absogrow AI. I found [business] on Google Maps — [rating] stars from [n] patients/guests, which is excellent. I looked for your website and social pages and couldn't find any, so someone searching for [a dentist] in [town] only sees a pin on the map. Where do most of your new [patients] come from right now?"

For hotels listed only on booking sites: point out that Expedia-type sites take a commission on every booking and they have no direct channel of their own.

## Objections (from the playbook)
Acknowledge, associate ("that's a sensible question"), ask the next question. "Too expensive" = value not clear yet → rebuild the gap; offer smaller scope or terms, never a discount. "I need to think" → ask what they are weighing. "I'll ask my partner" → ask what their main concern would be.
