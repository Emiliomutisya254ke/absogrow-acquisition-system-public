# Results summary (anonymised)

Business names, websites and contact details are left out of this public version on purpose.

## Pilot — 20 Nov 2026
- 20 Nairobi businesses, five each in restaurants, real estate, dental clinics and fashion retail.
- About 40 minutes for 20 businesses. $0 spent.
- Grades: 0 A, 8 B, 12 C.
- 6 emails drafted, approved by the owner and sent.
- Pre-send re-check: of about 20 factual claims in the six drafts, 2 were wrong and were corrected before sending.

## Batches 2-4 — 21-23 Nov 2026
Sector focus: dental clinics, opticians, hotels, in towns across Kenya.

| Batch | Businesses | A | B | C |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 15 | 1 | 10 | 4 |
| 3 | 15 | 5 | 7 | 3 |
| 4 | 15 | 3 | 9 | 3 |

- 65 businesses audited in total, with every PageSpeed test in batches 2-4 producing a score.
- Common findings: slow mobile load times (main content often 15-25 s), missing page titles and descriptions, no analytics or ad pixel, social icons that link nowhere, no tap-to-call or WhatsApp link.
- Not drafted: businesses with only a contact form or no email, sites already agency-built, and strong sites with little to fix.

## Lessons
- Re-check every absence claim ("no WhatsApp", "no social links") in the full page source; widgets and structured data hide links.
- Wait for the page to finish loading before counting anything; slow sites need a source fetch.
- Outside Nairobi, most dental clinics and opticians have no website at all. That is a lead type of its own.
- Finding businesses through web search favours those that already rank; Places API discovery would widen the net.
- Scripted search triggers bot checks; do lookups one at a time and stop when challenged.
