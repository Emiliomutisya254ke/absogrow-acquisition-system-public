# Scoring rubric (v1)

Two independent scores, each out of 100. **The lead grade comes from the commercial opportunity score.** A high score never authorises outreach by itself; contact eligibility is separate.

## Digital presence score (strength of what they have now; higher = stronger)
| Part | Points |
|---|---|
| Website quality and UX (loads, mobile, speed, navigation) | 20 |
| SEO (title, description, headings, structured data, alt text) | 20 |
| Social media quality (linked profiles, completeness, activity seen) | 20 |
| Brand consistency | 15 |
| Content strategy | 15 |
| Conversion opportunities (call, WhatsApp, booking, forms) | 10 |

## Commercial opportunity score (quality as a lead; higher = better lead)
| Part | Points |
|---|---|
| Identified growth opportunity (clear, fixable problems) | 20 |
| Potential business value (size, branches, price point) | 20 |
| Social media opportunity | 15 |
| Website and SEO opportunity | 15 |
| Business growth potential | 10 |
| Contact accessibility (a usable generic channel) | 10 |
| Fit with Absogrow AI services | 10 |

## Grades
| Grade | Commercial score |
|---|---|
| A | 80-100 |
| B | 65-79 |
| C | 45-64 |
| Low priority | below 45 |

## Notes from use
- Business value and growth potential are judgements from visible positioning; they are the least reliable parts.
- A business with a strong existing site (often agency-built) scores low on opportunity and is usually not drafted.
- Every finding stores: what was observed, evidence URL, why it matters, recommended fix, service, confidence.
